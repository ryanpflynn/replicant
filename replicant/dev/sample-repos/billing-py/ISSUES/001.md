---
title: Invoice tax is charged on the discount
labels: [replicant]
---
When an invoice has a percentage discount, `Invoice.tax_cents` computes tax on the gross subtotal instead of the
taxable amount (subtotal minus discount), so discounted customers are over-charged tax.

Example: one line of EUR 100.00, a 10 % discount and 20 % tax.

- expected: discount EUR 10.00, tax EUR 18.00 (20 % of 90.00), total EUR 108.00
- actual: tax EUR 20.00, total EUR 110.00

```python
inv = Invoice([Line("plan", 10000)], discount_percent=Decimal(10), tax_rate=Decimal("0.2"))
inv.tax_cents()  # 2000, want 1800
inv.total_cents()  # 11000, want 10800
```

Compute tax on `taxable_cents()`, rounded half-even with `billing.money.round_cents`, and add a test for a
discounted, taxed invoice. An invoice without a discount must keep its current totals.
