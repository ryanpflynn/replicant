---
title: Refund ledger entries double-count FX fees for partial refunds
labels: [replicant]
---
When a charge that carried an FX fee is partially refunded, `ledger.ApplyRefund` posts an
`fx_fee_reversal` entry for the **whole** FX fee instead of the share attributable to the refunded amount.
Refunding twice (or a partial refund followed by a full one) reverses the fee more than once, so the
customer is credited more FX fee than they paid.

Example: charge EUR 100.00 with an FX fee of EUR 0.48, partial refund of EUR 40.00.

- expected: `fx_fee_reversal` = EUR -0.19 (0.48 × 40/100 = 0.192, rounded half-even to the cent)
- actual: `fx_fee_reversal` = EUR -0.48

The failing test is `TestApplyRefund/partial_eur_fx_fee` in `internal/ledger/ledger_test.go`:

```
go test -count=1 -run '^TestApplyRefund$/^partial_eur_fx_fee$' ./internal/ledger
--- FAIL: TestApplyRefund/partial_eur_fx_fee
    fx_fee_reversal amount = EUR -48, want EUR -19
```

Prorate the FX fee reversal with the existing `ledger.Prorate(amount, part, whole)` helper (integer
arithmetic, half-even rounding). Do not compute the ratio in float64. A full refund must still reverse
the whole fee.
