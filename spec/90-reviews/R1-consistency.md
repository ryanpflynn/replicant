The findings cite 135 distinct XD entries in the Contract column. Most are entries where the named provider has not taken up the assumption, or has taken it up in an incompatible shape. §6 lists the XD entries checked and found satisfied.

**What breaks first:** a job cannot run end to end as specified. The blockers below include the following failures:
- Network policies stop the controller from reaching the Runner's internal mint and the grant JWKS.
- RBAC lets the controller neither create nor patch Pods.
- The controller watches namespaces by a label no one sets.
- Every canonical example fails Runner admission (A-8).
- Every QA pod with services is denied by the image-path rule.
- Every eval and utility run is denied by the network-profile list.
- Intake rejects every pinned, ramped or overridden job (`assignment_reason` CHECK).
- Shadow and eval scoring runs violate C-RUNNER and C-DEF.
- Memory curation rolls back on an audit FK.
- The local stack has no run grants at all.

The headline gaps are not in the frozen contracts, which are internally consistent and validate. They are at the seams between workstreams, where each side wrote its half of an interface independently and the XD log recorded an assumption that the provider never took up (§4, Contract column).
